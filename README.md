<h2 align="center">
How to Play
</h2>
The player's goal is to navigate through the maze from the start to the cheese at the end. Along the way the player can collect items to help them survive against the enemy rats and enemy bats that the maze contains.

***

* W/Up - Move/attack upwards
* A/Left - Move/attack left
* S/Down - Move/attack downwards
* D/Right - Move/attack right
* Space - Ready attack
* R - Reset level
* ESC - Open/close pause menu
***
